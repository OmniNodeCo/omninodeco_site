Infrastructure people can stop thinking about

We're a distributed team of 60 engineers, operators and designers who believe the network should be the boring part — so your users never notice it.

Our mission

Distance is a bug.
We're patching it.

Every millisecond between your code and your user is a tax on quality. OmniNodeCo started in 2021 when three engineers got tired of explaining why a "global" app felt slow in the other hemisphere.

Today we operate 42 edge regions on our own hardware, run everything we ship, and publish every incident. We're profitable, independent, and hiring.

Story

How we got here

2021

Founded in a Berlin basement

Three engineers, one rented rack in Frankfurt and a very opinionated manifesto: "The network should feel local, everywhere."

2022

OmniCore goes live

Our container orchestrator runs production traffic for our first 12 customers. Uptime: 99.98% — not good enough, we said.

2023

20 regions, 1 control plane

EdgeMesh reaches 20 regions and we open the public API. FluxDB ingests its first billion metrics in a single day.

2024

FluxDB 2.0 & Sentinel

Rewritten in Rust, FluxDB 2.0 cuts query latency by 60%. Sentinel blocks its first 1 Tbps DDoS attack.

2025

42 regions, 60 people

The mesh covers six continents. Cortex brings sub-10 ms ML inference to the edge. The team grows to 60 across 14 countries.

2026

This website ships via GitHub Actions → FTP

Even our marketing site runs the same push-to-deploy discipline as the platform. Dogfooding, always.

Values

What we optimize for

Latency is a feature

We measure everything in milliseconds and ship nothing that doesn't make the network faster or more reliable.

Dogfood or die

Every service we offer runs on our own stack, on our own hardware, with the same API you get.

Radical transparency

Public status, public postmortems, public roadmaps. If we break something, you'll hear it from us first.
