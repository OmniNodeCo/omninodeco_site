FAQ

Quick answers

How fast can I deploy to the edge?

If your repo is already on GitHub, connect it and pick regions — most teams run their first production workload within 30 minutes. Push-to-deploy keeps it that way forever.

Do you really operate your own hardware?

Yes. We lease and operate infrastructure in 42 colocation facilities on six continents. No third-party cloud in the path between your users and your workloads.

What does pricing look like?

The free tier includes 5 regions and 50 GB-month of edge compute. Paid plans start at $49/month and scale with usage — no long-term contracts, no egress fees.

Do you offer support for migrating from another cloud?

Yes — our solutions team runs free migration workshops. Most teams move without downtime using our built-in traffic mirroring.

How does this website get deployed?

This very site is a static site published with GitHub Actions over FTP. Every push to main syncs it to production using credentials stored as encrypted GitHub secrets.

Prefer to try it first?

Spin up a sandbox project in the free tier. Five regions, real traffic, no credit card.
